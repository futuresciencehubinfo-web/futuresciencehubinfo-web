## 🌐 ABOUT FUTURE SCIENCE HUB

> **Future Science Hub (FSH)** is a technology and STEM-focused initiative
> dedicated to practical learning, innovation and real-world engineering projects.

We work with:

- 🔬 **Science & STEM Education**
- 🤖 **Robotics**
- ⚡ **IoT & Smart Automation**
- 🧠 **AI & Technology**
- 🔌 **Embedded Systems**
- 🚁 **Drone Technology**
- 🖨️ **3D Printing**
- 🏠 **Home Automation**
- 💻 **Software & Web Projects**
- 🛠️ **DIY Electronics**

### 🎯 Our Vision

> **Learn Technology. Build Solutions. Create the Future.**
